COVER IMAGES FOR THE "VIEW MORE" PROJECTS
-----------------------------------------
These files are matched to the cards by EXACT name
(.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp all work):

  project-04   ->  YouTube video      (youtu.be/ZT8ssYDq8KM)
  project-05   ->  YouTube video      (youtu.be/srEx5fPw7M4)
  project-06   ->  Instagram reel     (APSI CHECK Summit 2026)
  project-07   ->  Instagram reel     (Petroleum Technology Development Fund)

To replace an image: overwrite the file, keeping the same name.
A card with no matching file shows an "Image coming soon" placeholder.

Cards display at 16:9 (landscape). 04 and 05 are landscape and fit as-is.
06 and 07 are vertical, so they are cropped to a landscape window; move that window
with `imagePosition` on the matching entry in src/lib/content.js
(e.g. "50% 22%" = window sits 22% of the way down the image).

For the sharpest result use images at least 1280px wide.
