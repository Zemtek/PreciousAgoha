COVER IMAGES FOR THE "VIEW MORE" PROJECTS
-----------------------------------------
Drop your images in this folder using EXACTLY these names
(.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp all work):

  project-04   ->  YouTube video      (youtu.be/ZT8ssYDq8KM)
  project-05   ->  YouTube video      (youtu.be/srEx5fPw7M4)
  project-06   ->  Instagram reel     (instagram.com/reel/DcyUYqxAE2h)
  project-07   ->  Instagram reel     (instagram.com/reel/DdrvzMuMvg1)

Example:  src/assets/projects/project-06.jpg

The card swaps from the placeholder to your image automatically.
Cards display at 16:9 (landscape), so use a landscape image
(1280x720 or larger). Vertical reel covers will be centre-cropped.
